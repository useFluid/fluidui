# Code of conduct

Fluid is made by people who care about motion, accessibility and each other. In issues, pull requests and anywhere else the project lives:

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique the work, never the person.
- Welcome newcomers and answer the question that was asked.
- No harassment, insults, sexual content or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Respect a "no". If someone asks you to stop, stop.

If someone breaks these rules, report it privately through the contact in [SECURITY](SECURITY.md). Reports are read by the maintainer only. People who break the rules may be warned, have their comments removed, or be blocked from the project.
